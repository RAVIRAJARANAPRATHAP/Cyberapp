DOCUMENT: How to Report Cybercrime in India

This document explains what to do immediately after a cybercrime, how to report it, and
what information to keep ready. Keep this information somewhere accessible.

---

STEP 1 — ACT IMMEDIATELY IF MONEY WAS TRANSFERRED

Time is critical. If you or someone you know has transferred money to a fraudster:

1. Call your bank's 24x7 fraud helpline right away and say: "I have been the victim of
   a cyber fraud and I want to freeze the fraudulent transaction."
   - SBI: 1800-11-2211
   - HDFC: 1800-202-6161
   - ICICI: 1800-1080
   - Other banks: the number is printed on the back of your debit card.

2. Call the National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930
   This helpline is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Tell the operator what
   happened and they will guide you on next steps. The earlier you call, the better the
   chance of the transaction being reversed.

---

STEP 2 — FILE AN ONLINE COMPLAINT

Visit: cybercrime.gov.in
Click "File a Complaint" and select the relevant category:
- Financial Fraud / Online Financial Fraud
- Cyber Blackmail / Threatening
- Other Cyber Crimes

You will need to create a free account to file the complaint. The process takes about
10–15 minutes. You will receive a complaint number that you can use to track progress.

---

STEP 3 — FILE A LOCAL POLICE COMPLAINT

Visit your nearest police station and ask to file an FIR (First Information Report) for
cybercrime. Many states also have dedicated Cybercrime Police Stations in district
headquarters.

Keep a copy of the FIR acknowledgment for your records.

---

WHAT INFORMATION TO KEEP READY BEFORE REPORTING

Collect and save the following before making the complaint — do not delete any messages
or call records:

- Date, time, and duration of the fraudulent call/message
- Phone number(s) used by the fraudster (even if it appears to be a known number)
- Screenshots of any WhatsApp messages, SMS, or chat conversations
- Screenshots of any fake websites or apps shown to you
- Bank transaction details: amount, date, and the UPI ID / account number money was
  sent to
- Any names, "badge numbers," or "employee IDs" the fraudster mentioned
- The app name if you were asked to install anything (AnyDesk, Quick Support, etc.)

---

OTHER USEFUL CONTACTS

- PM Cyber Safety Awareness: stay updated at cyberdost.mha.gov.in
- Report SMS spam / fraudulent numbers to the telecom regulator: sancharsaathi.gov.in
- SEBI (for investment fraud): scores.gov.in | 1800-22-7575
- RBI (for banking fraud): rbi.org.in complaint portal | 14440

---

IMPORTANT REMINDERS

- No government agency charges a fee over the phone.
- No bank asks for OTP, CVV, or passwords — ever.
- "Digital arrest" is not a real legal concept in India.
- Reporting quickly is the single most important thing you can do.

National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930
Online Portal: cybercrime.gov.in
