SCAM TYPE: Fake Investment / Stock-Tip Scam

HOW IT WORKS:
Victims are added without consent to WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, or contacted on
social media by people claiming to be SEBI-registered advisors, stock market experts, or
representatives of well-known broking firms (like Zerodha, Angel One, etc.). Sometimes
they use the name and photo of a real celebrity or financial personality to appear
credible.

The fraudsters begin by sharing a few "free tips" that appear to be accurate — these
early tips are fabricated to build trust, not real market insight. Once trust is
established, they introduce a "private high-return investment opportunity" — typically a
fake trading app, a fraudulent website, or an unregistered scheme promising guaranteed
monthly returns of 20–50%.

Victims are asked to deposit money into the platform. Initially, the dashboard shows
large profits to encourage further investment. When the victim tries to withdraw, they
are told to pay additional "taxes," "TDS charges," or "account unblocking fees" before
release of funds. The money is never returned and the fraudsters eventually disappear.

TYPICAL SCRIPT USED:
"I am a SEBI-certified analyst. Our exclusive group has made 300% returns this year.
Invest just Rs. 10,000 and we guarantee Rs. 50,000 in 30 days. Limited spots available."

RED FLAGS:
- SEBI-registered advisors are prohibited by law from guaranteeing any fixed return on
  investment. Anyone who guarantees profits is either lying or operating illegally.
- Legitimate investment platforms are registered and regulated — always check SEBI's
  official intermediary list at sebi.gov.in before investing.
- Unsolicited additions to "investment tip" WhatsApp or Telegram groups are a classic
  fraud setup.
- Fake apps and websites often have no SEBI registration number, or show a number that
  does not match the SEBI registry.
- Being asked to pay a fee to "unlock" your own profits or "clear taxes" before
  withdrawal is a clear sign the platform is fraudulent.
- High-pressure language — "offer closes tonight," "only 5 spots left" — is
  manipulation, not legitimate sales.

WHAT TO DO:
- Never invest in platforms or schemes suggested by someone who contacted you out of the
  blue, especially on WhatsApp or Telegram.
- Verify any advisor's SEBI registration number on sebi.gov.in before committing money.
- Do not pay any additional fee to "release" money you have already invested — you will
  not recover it by paying more.
- If you have already invested, stop sending money immediately, save all chat records and
  screenshots, and report right away.

WHERE TO REPORT:
- National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930 (call, available 24x7)
- Report online at: cybercrime.gov.in
- SEBI's SCORES portal for investment fraud: scores.gov.in
- Inform your bank to flag any transactions made to the fraudulent platform.
