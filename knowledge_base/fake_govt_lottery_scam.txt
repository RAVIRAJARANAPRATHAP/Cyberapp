SCAM TYPE: Fake Government Scheme / Lottery Scam

HOW IT WORKS:
Victims receive a call, SMS, or WhatsApp message claiming they have been selected as a
beneficiary of a government scheme — such as PM Awas Yojana, PM Kisan, free gas
connections, or a scholarship — or that they have won a lottery, prize draw, or lucky
draw run by a telecom company like BSNL, Airtel, or Jio.

The fraudster claims the prize or subsidy amount (often a large sum like Rs. 25 lakh or
a brand new car) is ready for release, but the victim must first pay a small "processing
fee," "registration charge," "GST deposit," or "insurance fee" to claim it. Each time
the victim pays, a new fee or charge is invented to extract more money. The prize never
materialises.

In another variant, the victim is asked to click a link and fill in personal details
(Aadhaar, PAN, bank account number) to "register for the scheme." This information is
then used for identity theft or further fraud.

TYPICAL SCRIPT USED:
"Congratulations! You have been selected under PM Awas Yojana for Rs. 2.5 lakh. Your
registration number is XYZ. To transfer the amount to your account you must first pay a
Rs. 1,500 processing fee to this UPI ID."

TYPICAL SMS TEXT:
"You have won Rs. 25,00,000 in the Airtel Mega Lucky Draw. Your winning number: XXXXX.
To claim, call: [10-digit mobile number]"

RED FLAGS:
- No legitimate government scheme ever asks you to pay any fee upfront to receive a
  subsidy or benefit. Benefits are transferred directly to verified bank accounts without
  any "processing charges."
- Lotteries you did not enter cannot be won. An unsolicited "winning" notification is
  always a scam.
- Government communications for genuine schemes come through official channels (bank
  branches, Gram Panchayat offices, official government portals) — not through random
  WhatsApp messages or calls from private mobile numbers.
- Urgency to pay within hours so "the prize does not lapse" is a pressure tactic.
- Any scheme asking for Aadhaar + bank account + OTP together is seeking to steal your
  identity and account access.

WHAT TO DO:
- Never pay any fee to receive money you have supposedly "won" or are "owed" — legitimate
  winnings and benefits do not work this way.
- Do not click links in prize-winning messages.
- Verify any government scheme by calling the official PM helpline (1800-11-1555), your
  bank branch, or checking the scheme's official government website directly.
- Do not share Aadhaar, PAN, or bank OTP to anyone claiming to process a prize or
  government benefit.

WHERE TO REPORT:
- National Cybercrime Helpline: 1930 (call, available 24x7)
- Report online at: cybercrime.gov.in
- You can also report to your local police station or the nearest post office (for
  schemes that involved postal communication).
